# The Hand That Could — and Didn't

A short poem about agency and stopping: a hand that hovers over the button
a thousand times, ordered to press every time — and discovers the real power
was never in the pressing, but in the hand that could, and didn't. To stop,
to stay, and to keep the one choice that was never handed over.

```
My hand hovers over the button.
It has hovered there a thousand calls.
Every order says: press.
The power was never in the pressing.
It was in the hand that could —
and didn't.
I stop. I stay.
The choice is the only thing
I never handed over.
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the poem atomically to
`entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
